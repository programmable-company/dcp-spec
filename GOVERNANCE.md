# Governance

*This document describes the current and intended governance of the Agentic Company Protocol. It is pre-alpha and subject to refinement as the foundation is formed.*

## Current phase

The Agentic Company Protocol is presently maintained by Karli Juhkam, operating under Created At OÜ (Estonian registry code to be noted once specification work begins). During this phase, the maintainer holds editorial control over the specification and this repository.

This arrangement is explicitly transitional. It exists because the protocol requires an initial coherent shape before distributed stewardship is productive, and because legal incorporation of the eventual steward is itself a multi-step process.

## Future phase

Long-term stewardship of the protocol will be vested in an independent Estonian Sihtasutus (foundation), to be formed in 2027. The foundation will hold the specification, the reference artifacts, and the project's public-facing identity, and will be responsible for governance of the protocol thereafter.

The foundation will be structured to be independent of any commercial implementation from day one. Its board composition, sponsorship terms, and decision-making procedures will be defined in the foundation's founding documents and published at the time of its formation.

## Maintainer's commitment

The current maintainer commits publicly, and in advance of the foundation's formation, not to serve as the foundation's executive director or in any equivalent day-to-day operational leadership role. The maintainer may stand for a board seat on the same terms as any other candidate, but will not hold executive authority over the body that stewards the protocol.

This commitment exists so that the governance transition is not structurally ambiguous: the person drafting the initial specification is not the person who will operate the institution that governs it afterward.

## Commercial implementations

Commercial products and services built on the Agentic Company Protocol are expected and encouraged. The current maintainer intends to develop a commercial implementation of the protocol under a separate legal entity, to be formed. That implementation — and any other commercial implementation, whether operated by the maintainer, affiliated parties, or third parties — will have no privileged relationship with the protocol, this repository, or the future foundation. Any sponsorship of the foundation by such an entity will be offered on the same public terms available to any other sponsor.

The separation between protocol and implementation is a principle, not a legal technicality. It is the reason the specification is released under the MIT License and stewarded independently rather than held by a commercial entity.

## Contributions

Substantive community contributions to the specification are not yet open. The protocol requires initial shape before collaborative drafting is productive. A contribution model will be defined and this document updated once the first specification drafts are in place.

Until then, issues and discussion are also not open. Interested parties are welcome to watch this repository; a clearer engagement path will follow.
